# World Models — MDN-RNN latent dynamics (Ha and Schmidhuber, 2018)
Scientific source: https://worldmodels.github.io/ , sections MDN-RNN (M) Model, Controller (C) Model and Putting Everything Together. Original author SVG: https://worldmodels.github.io/assets/mdn_rnn_new.svg .
Scope: the M component's stochastic latent dynamics, not an invented full project overview and not a modern Transformer/diffusion model. VAE Vision and Controller exist in the broader project, but this figure focuses on the genuinely published MDN-RNN mechanism. z is a latent observation produced by the separate VAE; a is an action selected externally; h is the recurrent state.
At time t, the current latent z_t, action a_t and previous recurrent state h_t condition an LSTM recurrent update. The updated state h_{t+1} supports a mixture-density output head that predicts a probability distribution of the next latent z_{t+1}. The paper expresses this predictive law as P(z_{t+1} | a_t, z_t, h_t). M predicts a distribution, not a single deterministic future. The mixture head outputs parameters of a mixture of Gaussians, conventionally mixture weights pi, means mu and standard deviations sigma. Temperature tau adjusts model uncertainty during sampling. A sampled next latent is a prediction, not a measured future observation.
The author SVG unrolls the same recurrent mechanism over successive time steps. RNN cells carry h horizontally; each time's z and a provide conditioning; an MDN head above predicts the next latent, with a temperature mark.
