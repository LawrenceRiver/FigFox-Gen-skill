# Short generation brief

Use the following as a scaffold, populated with this paper's actual evidence. Keep it short enough to guide the generator.

> Create a complete academic poster for the supplied paper. Primary reference: [actual user template/reference, or recorded selected reference]. Use its declared visual role without copying its research content, logos or authors.
>
> Tell the scientific story: problem, input, output, model/method, innovation, key evidence and essential limits. Use the approved substantive copy and exact numerical conditions below. Retain useful explanatory prose; do not reduce the paper to slogans or rely on tiny text for density.
>
> Draw every planned figure/table with its full contents: labels, arrows, axes, legend, values, units and comparison context. No blank frames, reserved slots, placeholder labels or missing visuals. Give the main method and strongest results adequate space. For each source visual provide its actual pixel dimensions, aspect ratio and planned display frame; allocate space from that ratio before prose. Preserve source aspect or explicitly contain the full figure within the frame. Attached evidence and textual specifications are authoritative; do not invent data.
>
> Keep related text and figures adjacent, visual alignment consistent, and text inside its frames. Use available conventional fonts with a clear hierarchy, clean shared backgrounds, and essential structural rules. Declare per-module area/text balance, section-band/background colors and every intended dot/bullet/rule in the visual roster; these are design content, not disposable styling. Avoid unrequested gray microcopy and decorative pseudo-explanations. Do not stretch scientific figures along one axis to fill a rectangle; preserve aspect and adjust the composition.
>
> [Exact header and author information from the paper.]
> [Approved copy and source-linked visual instructions per section.]
> [Attachment list, each with a distinct role.]

## Before calling the image tool

- The initial report/callback decisions are resolved for all scientific blockers.
- Required technical story, exact comparison settings and useful figures are present.
- The user's primary template is passed to the tool when supported; otherwise disclose the limitation.
- Attachments obey the tool's actual current limit. For unattachable evidence, include exact complete labels/relations/data in the prompt. If the tool cannot represent necessary evidence faithfully, disclose that constraint rather than silently omit it.
- Every planned figure has measured PDF/pixel size, source hash and ratio plus an intended display frame in the saved prompt/manifest. An unmatched frame ratio explicitly uses contain, not image stretching.
- Planned modules and their area/text balance, colors, dots/bullets/rules are enumerated for later fidelity checking.
- The prompt and actual attachment paths are saved once. Nothing requests later reconstruction or repair.

## Retry budget

One initial call plus at most one regeneration. Receive and save the actual output; notice obvious missing visuals or global scientific failure during that intake, without launching a separate reviewer agent or elaborate scoring stage. Retry only with the concrete responsible correction. Minor aesthetic differences do not justify another call. Unresolved serious faults after call two are reported as unresolved; the run is not labeled approved. Do not start a third call or a repair pipeline.

The delivered PNG is a model-rendered artifact. Source-backed planning reduces errors but does not certify the final pixels. Do not claim OCR validation, official benchmark scores, an editable vector deliverable or a measured turnaround time unless those were actually established.
